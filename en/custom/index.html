What Nkapweb delivers as standard in a custom project

Client portal

Every Nkapweb client already has a basic client area at nkapweb.com/client (French): e-mail link sign-in, order and payment tracking. Custom projects extend it to your own customers:

  • Login by e-mail link or WhatsApp code, no password to remember
  • Order and project tracking, step by step
  • Downloadable invoices and receipts, payment history
  • Shared documents: contracts, mockups, acceptance reports
  • Change requests with tracking
  • Access to training sessions and recordings

Included in the foundation

SYSCOHADA accounting

  • Automatic entries for every confirmed payment: cash at net value (accounts 52 / 538 per operator), operator fee as an expense (631), deposit (4191), customer (411), revenue (70x), 19.25% VAT (4431) if registered
  • Invoicing on delivery with deposits applied
  • Sales, cash and general journals, general ledger, trial balance
  • Reconciliation of Orange Money, MoMo and bank statements
  • Exports for your chartered accountant, statements for the SYSCOHADA minimal cash-basis system (SMT)
  • Designed by an OHADA accounting professional, configurable to your chart of accounts
