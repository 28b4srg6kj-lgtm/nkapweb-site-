I do not have a bank card. How do I pay?

With your Orange Money or MTN Mobile Money account. You receive a link and confirm on your phone. The receipt arrives on WhatsApp and by e-mail.

Does the site really belong to me?

Yes. Once the balance is paid, the domain name, content, logins and code are yours. The design templates remain Nkapweb's property.

What happens if I am not satisfied?

You approve the site on a test address before paying the balance. Until it is approved, the revisions included in the pack apply. The deposit covers the design work done.

I live in Europe. How does it work?

You pay by bank card or SEPA transfer, in euros, on an invoice in euros. The site can target customers back home, with FCFA payment built in.

Is the GIMAC card accepted everywhere?

GIMAC cards are accepted through our partner banks in the CEMAC zone. Tell us your bank when you ask for a quote: we confirm the channel before sending the link.

And after one year, what about hosting?

Domain and hosting are renewed at cost, about 25 000 FCFA per year, or included in the subscription and maintenance. You are told one month ahead.

Can I switch from a subscription to a purchase?

Yes, at any time. Months already paid are deducted from the pack price, up to six months.

What if I do not provide copy and photos?

We write and choose visuals with you, as an option. The delivery time starts when the deposit and the content are received.
